Meet Our Studio

Defining Spaces, Building Trust

"Designed Your Way. Felt Every Day."

HUMYNEX STUDIO is a multi-award-winning architectural and interior design firm. We design timeless residential and commercial spaces that embody individuality, enhance comfort, and inspire connection. Every project is thoughtfully crafted to reflect the character, aspirations, and lifestyle of those who experience it.

Our team consists of passionate architects, interior stylists, space planners, and visualizers who collaborate to deliver fully execution-ready blueprints and bespoke turnkey transformations.

Our Expertise

Tailored Design Solutions

Architectural Planning

Bespoke concept drawings, master layouts, elevations, and structural integrations tailored for luxury villas and custom residences.

Bespoke Interior Design

Complete curation of furniture, specialized light designs, custom materials, color consulting, and detailed styling for luxury residences.

Corporate Office Design

Modern, agile, and productive work layouts designed to support employee well-being and inspire dynamic corporate collaboration.

Personalized Home Design

Personalized styling for private chambers, kitchens, dressing suites, and family spaces reflecting functional elegance.

Optimized Space Planning

Deep structural studies to maximize light penetration, spatial circulation, and utility distribution for complex developments.

Turnkey Execution Solutions

End-to-end design, construction, structural inspection, materials procurement, and seamless handover with absolute budget controls.

Our Journey

The Design Process

01

Consultation

We listen to your ideas, budget requirements, and expectations for the project.

02

Concept

We develop initial spatial layouts, mood boards, and aesthetic direction.

03

Planning

Detailed blueprint maps, electrical maps, and materials schedules are designed.

04

Visuals

Interactive 3D renderings and material boards are presented for final approvals.

05

Execution

On-site structural build, interior installations, and styling begins under supervision.

06

Handover

We clean, inspect, dress, and hand over the key to your extraordinary new space.
